Community Health Score

Every community gets a Health Score from 0–100, calculated monthly. Higher scores earn more reward revenue.

What makes up your score

  • Active members (30%) — real members doing real things
  • Original content (25%) — posts, guides, tutorials you wrote
  • Meaningful discussions (15%) — comments longer than 20 characters
  • New ideas (10%) — challenges, proposals, solutions
  • Member contributions (10%) — resources, accepted solutions
  • Retention (10%) — how many members come back

Score ranges

  • 70–100 · Excellent — top reward tier
  • 50–69 · Good — eligible for rewards
  • 30–49 · Growing — building toward eligibility
  • 0–29 · Needs work — not eligible

Monetization Requirements

  • Account verified (email + phone)
  • Community established (14+ days, 10+ members)
  • 20+ active members monthly
  • 40%+ original content rate
  • Active 7+ days in the last 30
  • No serious violations
  • No suspicious engagement
  • Payment info verified
  • Fraud checks passed

How Rewards Split

👑

50% Owner

The community owner receives half of the community's monthly reward.

🏆

50% Contributors

Split among the top 10 contributors by verified contribution score.
