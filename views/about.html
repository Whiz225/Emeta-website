About PiiChat

PiiChat is a comprehensive social communication platform built by Emeta. It brings together messaging, calling, and social networking in one seamless experience.

Our Mission

To connect people through innovative, secure, and easy-to-use communication tools. We believe staying in touch should be effortless, private, and enjoyable.

What PiiChat Offers

  • Instant Messaging – Real-time chat with read receipts and encryption
  • Voice & Video Calls – High-quality calling with WebRTC
  • Social Feed – Share posts, photos, and status updates
  • Virtual Gifting – Send animated gifts to friends
  • Smart Notifications – Real-time push notifications
  • Privacy & Security – End-to-end encryption and secure login

Developer

PiiChat is developed by Emeta, an independent mobile app developer based in Nigeria.
