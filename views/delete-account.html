Delete Your PiiChat Account

Effective Date: September 15, 2026

If you would like to delete your PiiChat account and all associated data, please follow the steps below.

Option 1: Delete In-App (Fastest)

  1. Open the PiiChat app on your device.
  2. Go to your Profile.
  3. Scroll down and tap "Delete Account".
  4. Confirm the deletion.
  5. Your account and all associated data will be permanently deleted within 30 days.

Option 2: Request via Email

If you cannot access the app, please send an email to:

nwosuemmanuel1994@gmail.com

Include the following information:

  • Your registered email address
  • Your registered phone number
  • Subject line: "Account Deletion Request"

We will process your request within 7 business days.

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • Your profile information (name, email, phone number)
  • Your profile picture and cover photo
  • All your messages and chat history
  • All your posts and status updates
  • All your gift transactions
  • Your device tokens and notification settings

Data That May Be Retained

  • Anonymous, aggregated data that no longer identifies you
  • Data required for legal compliance (retained as long as required by law)

Data Retention Period

  • User-provided data: Deleted within 30 days of request
  • Automatically collected data: Deleted within 90 days
  • Aggregated/anonymous data: Retained indefinitely
