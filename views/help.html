Frequently Asked Questions

How do I delete my account?

You can delete your account from within the app (Profile → Delete Account) or by following the steps on our Delete Account page.

Is my data encrypted?

Yes. All messages are end-to-end encrypted. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I report a user?

Open the user's profile and tap the "..." menu. Or email us at nwosuemmanuel1994@gmail.com .

How do I recover a deleted message?

Deleted messages cannot be recovered. Be careful when deleting.
